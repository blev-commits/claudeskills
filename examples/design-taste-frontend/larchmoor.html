What is actually there.

In every cabin

  • Wood stove and a full storeSplit larch, kindling and matches, topped up before you arrive.
  • Hot water off the stoveLight it when you get in and there is a bath by the evening.
  • Gas ring and a proper kitchenPans, knives, a grinder and a stovetop pot for coffee.
  • Oil lamps and candlesThere is no mains power. Torches are by the door.

On the estate

  • Paths onto the plateauStraight out of the door and up. Maps in each cabin.
  • Deer, capercaillie, eaglesKeep to the paths between April and July for nesting.
  • Parking at AchleanLeave the car there. We carry bags up on the quad if you ask.
  • No signal, radio in each cabinIt reaches the estate office, staffed from seven until nine.
